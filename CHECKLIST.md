## How findings are graded

| | Bucket | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 🔴 | **Urgent Safety Issue** | Potential safety concern requiring prompt attention. |
| 🟣 | **Recommended Repair** | Action recommended in the near future. |
| 🟡 | **Monitor** | Monitor over next 6-12 months. |
| 🟢 | **Looks Great** | No action required. |

Red and Purple are *opportunities* — they get a budget range and a follow-up.
Yellow and Green never carry a number.

## Interior Review

### Doors & Windows

**Interior doors**  
Open and close a few interior doors — sticking, rubbing, latch not catching, loose hinges?

**Window operation & seals**  
Check a window per elevation — hard to open, failed seal, damaged sash, drafty?

**Exterior door weatherstripping**  
Look for daylight around exterior doors. Check sweeps and thresholds.

### Kitchen & Baths

**Tub, shower & sink caulking**  
Check caulk lines at tub, shower and sink backsplash — cracked, mouldy, pulling away?

**Tile & grout**  
Any cracked, missing or crumbling grout? Loose tiles?

**Under-sink plumbing** ⚠️ *safety-sensitive*  
Open the cabinets under kitchen and bath sinks — active drips, water staining, swollen cabinet base?

**Cabinets & drawers**  
Sagging doors, worn hinges, drawer slides binding, loose pulls?

**Toilet stability & seal**  
Rock the toilet gently — loose at the floor? Any staining at the base?

### Walls & Ceilings

**Wall & ceiling condition**  
Nail pops, seam cracks, dings, prior patch work showing through?

**Ceiling water staining** ⚠️ *safety-sensitive*  
Look up in the top-floor rooms and under bathrooms — any brown ring or bubbling?

**Trim & baseboards**  
Separated mitres, gaps at the wall, damaged or missing sections?

**Wall-mounted TV & shelving** ⚠️ *safety-sensitive*  
Anything mounted that shouldn't be — TV on drywall anchors, sagging shelves, unsecured furniture?

### Safety & Systems

**Smoke & CO alarms** ⚠️ *safety-sensitive*  
One per level and outside sleeping areas? Test button. Check the date on the back — 10 years is end of life.

**GFCI outlets in wet areas** ⚠️ *safety-sensitive*  
Kitchen, baths, laundry, garage — GFCI present? Press test/reset.

**Light fixtures & fans**  
Wobbling ceiling fans, loose fixtures, dead bulbs in hard-to-reach spots, missing cover plates?

### Floors & Stairs

**Handrails & stair treads** ⚠️ *safety-sensitive*  
Pull on every handrail. Loose balusters, squeaking or cupped treads?

**Flooring & transitions**  
Lifting planks, popped transition strips, soft spots underfoot, trip hazards?

## Exterior Review

### Roofline & Drainage

**Gutters & downspouts**  
Debris packed in, sagging runs, separated seams, downspouts discharging at the foundation?

**Fascia & soffit**  
Rot, peeling paint, open soffit panels, wildlife entry points?

### Entry & Siding

**Siding & exterior trim**  
Loose or missing pieces, soft trim at corners and windows, failed caulk joints?

**Front & back entry doors** ⚠️ *safety-sensitive*  
Door dragging, deadbolt not throwing fully, damaged jamb, worn threshold?

### Decks, Porches & Fences

**Deck & porch boards** ⚠️ *safety-sensitive*  
Walk it. Springy or split boards, protruding fasteners, rot at the ledger and posts?

**Deck & porch railings** ⚠️ *safety-sensitive*  
Push hard on every railing section. Loose posts, wide baluster gaps, rot at the base?

**Fence & gates**  
Leaning panels, dragging gates, failed hardware, rotted posts at grade?

### Grounds & Weatherproofing

**Exterior sealing & penetrations**  
Gaps at hose bibs, dryer vents, cable penetrations, foundation-to-siding transitions?

**Walkways & exterior steps** ⚠️ *safety-sensitive*  
Uneven or lifted sections, loose step treads, missing handrail at three or more risers?
